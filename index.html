How it is measured

The computation follows the Consumer Price Index Manual (2020). Same items from one period to the next, so that a price change is never confused with a change of product.

Why the Jevons formula

PJ = ∏ (pit / pit−1)1/n

The geometric mean of price relatives. It treats a doubling and a halving symmetrically. The Carli, an arithmetic mean, does not: when prices bounce, it drifts upwards with nothing having changed.
